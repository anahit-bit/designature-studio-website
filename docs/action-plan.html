The mobile app, in detail weeks 4 to 9

You chose the installable web app. It is the same site, installed with an icon on the home screen, working on a weak connection and able to send notifications. Here is what that means honestly.

Why this route

  • Feature parity is automatic. Everything on the desktop site is in the app the moment it ships, because it is the same site.
  • No Apple or Google developer accounts, no store review, no waiting on approvals.
  • No store commission on credit packs. A store app would hand Apple a cut of every purchase made inside it.
  • Roughly three weeks of build instead of three months, and one codebase to maintain instead of two.

What it costs you

  • On iPhone, notifications only work after the person adds the site to their home screen. That extra step will lose you a share of them.
  • You are not in the App Store, so there is no store search traffic and no store credibility signal.
  • Camera and photo handling are good but not as good as a native app would give you for room capture.

Notifications for you

  • Someone bought a credit pack.
  • Someone booked a consultation.
  • New feedback arrived.
  • A payment failed.

This is the part that pays for the whole phase. Right now you find out when you next open the admin page.

Notifications for customers

  • Your room is ready.
  • Your credits are running low.
  • Your consultation is tomorrow.
  • Your receipt.

No marketing blasts. The first promotional notification is the one that gets the app deleted.

A real store app becomes worth building when two things are true: people are asking for it, and notification opt in among your active users is running above roughly half. Until then it is three months of work for an icon you already have.

Every week, regardless the background cadence

Content

Two to four Journal posts a month, from the content calendar. Consistency beats volume. Submit every new URL in Search Console.

Pinterest

A few vertical pins a week, cut from real results, each linking back to an article. They compound quietly.

Reviews

Every happy client gets asked, once, for a Google or Trustpilot review. Both competitors are weak here.

Search Console

Ten minutes on a Friday. Which queries are climbing tells you what to write next.
