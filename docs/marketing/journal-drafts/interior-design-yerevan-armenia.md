## Body

### Interior design in Yerevan — and wherever you are
Designature is an interior design studio based in **Yerevan, Armenia**, working on apartments, houses,
and commercial spaces across Armenia — and with clients **worldwide, remotely**. What makes us
different from both a traditional local studio and a pure app: you get **AI tools to explore fast**
*and* **a real designer** to make it right.

### How it works
1. **Start free with AI.** Upload a photo of your room, take the [Style Quiz](/ai-vision), and
   [AI Vision](/ai-concepts) redesigns it in your style — with a shoppable list of real products.
2. **Add a designer when you want one.** Book a [$99 consultation](/consultation) (creditable toward a
   project) to refine the direction with an expert.
3. **Go all the way.** For full projects we handle concept, drawings, 3D, and materials — see
   [our services](/services) and [pricing](/pricing).

### Why a Yerevan studio, in three languages
We work in **Armenian, Russian, and English**, we understand what's actually available to buy and build
**here**, and we can serve you **in person in Yerevan or fully remotely**. That combination — local
knowledge, three languages, AI speed, and a human designer — is genuinely hard to find.

### What it costs
Start free. Paid AI tiers are **$19 and $49**, a designer consultation is **$99** (creditable), and
full projects are quoted to scope. See [pricing](/pricing) for details.
