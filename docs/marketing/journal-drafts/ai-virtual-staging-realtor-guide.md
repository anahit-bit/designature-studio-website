## Body

### The short version
Empty rooms photograph badly and sell slowly. **Physical staging** works but costs **$1,500–$5,000+
per room**. **AI virtual staging** furnishes a listing photo digitally for a **fraction** of that, in
minutes — and buyers increasingly expect it. The catch: you must **disclose** it and you must not use
it to hide a property's real condition.

### Physical vs virtual — when to use which
- **Vacant listing, tight timeline/budget:** virtual staging, every time.
- **Luxury / high-touch listing where buyers walk through often:** physical, or virtual for the photos + a light physical touch.
- **Occupied but dated:** virtual "restaging" (remove clutter, refresh) for the photos.

### The rules you can't skip (MLS disclosure)
Virtually staged photos **must be disclosed** — most MLSs require it, and some states (e.g. California's
AB 723) carry fines for undisclosed digital edits that could mislead. Practical rules:
1. **Label the image** "Virtually staged."
2. **Say so in the listing remarks.**
3. **Never edit out defects** (cracks, damage, water stains) or add features that aren't there. Stage
   the *potential*, don't fake the *property*. Preserve real walls, windows, and doors.

### How to stage a listing that actually sells
- Furnish for **broad appeal** (neutral, aspirational), not your personal taste.
- Stage the **rooms buyers care about**: living room, primary bedroom, kitchen/dining.
- Keep proportions real — furniture at true scale so buyers trust it.
- Add a **shoppable option** for buyers who love the look (a nice touch that also helps sellers picture living there).

## seo.faq[]
1. **Q:** How much does AI virtual staging cost in 2026?
   **A:** AI virtual staging tools range from free tiers to about $99/month; human virtual-staging services run roughly $16–$40 per image. That compares to $1,500–$5,000+ per room for physical staging.
2. **Q:** Is virtual staging legal, and do I have to disclose it?
   **A:** It's legal and widely used, but you must disclose it. Most MLSs require virtually staged photos to be labeled and noted in the listing remarks, and some states fine undisclosed edits that could mislead buyers. Never use staging to hide defects.
3. **Q:** Is virtual staging better than physical staging?
   **A:** For vacant listings on a budget or tight timeline, virtual staging wins on cost and speed. Physical staging still helps for luxury listings with frequent in-person showings. Many agents use virtual staging for the photos and a light physical touch in person.
4. **Q:** Will AI virtual staging change my property's layout?
   **A:** Good tools preserve the real architecture — walls, windows, and doors stay put. Designature's "True-Room Lock" keeps the structure fixed so photos represent the actual space.
5. **Q:** Can buyers actually buy the furniture in a staged photo?
   **A:** With some tools, yes — the staged items link to real, purchasable products. Designature can attach a curated shopping list to a staged room.
