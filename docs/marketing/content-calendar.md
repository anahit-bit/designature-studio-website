# GEO / SEO content calendar

The engine both MeltFlex and REimagine run: one page per high-intent query, structured for Google
*and* AI-answer citation. Below is the roadmap. Five flagship drafts are already written (see
`journal-drafts/`); the rest are ready-to-brief outlines — each should follow the same template:
**title · seo.metaTitle · seo.metaDescription · excerpt · body with H2s + a comparison/steps table ·
5–6 FAQ (feeds FAQPage schema) · internal links · a real image.**

## Already drafted (publish first)
1. **Best AI Interior Design Tools 2026** (honest comparison) — the GEO flagship.
2. **Rental-Friendly Interior Design** — rentals vertical + Pinterest anchor.
3. **AI Virtual Staging: A Realtor's Guide** — real-estate/non-brand lane REimagine ignores.
4. **Interior Design in Yerevan/Armenia** — local + trilingual white space.
5. *(this calendar)*

## Batch A — "vs" / comparison pages (intercept competitor brand demand)
Honest, useful, name competitors fairly; our edge (AI + human + curated shoppable + honest billing) shows on its own.
- Designature vs REimagine Home — for realtors weighing staging tools
- Designature vs MeltFlex AI — shoppable redesign, with vs without a designer
- Best **free** AI interior design tools (2026) — captures "free" intent, funnels to our free tier
- AI interior design vs hiring an interior designer — positions our hybrid as the answer

## Batch B — per-room (high volume, evergreen)
"AI [room] design ideas" + "how to design a [room]": living room, bedroom, kitchen, bathroom, home
office, small apartment, studio apartment, nursery, dining room, entryway. Each: ideas + before/after
+ "preview it free with AI Vision" CTA + FAQ. (Kitchen and small-apartment have the most search volume.)

## Batch C — per-style (Pinterest + AI-citation friendly)
"[Style] interior design guide" + "how to get the [style] look": Japandi, Scandinavian, Minimalist,
Mid-Century Modern, Boho, Coastal, Industrial, Farmhouse, Art Deco, Contemporary. Pair each with a
Pinterest board of AI before/afters.

## Batch D — real-estate / B2B (supports virtual staging)
- Virtual staging vs physical staging (cost/speed/rules)
- MLS virtual staging disclosure rules by state (evergreen, high-trust, linkable)
- Virtual staging for Airbnb / short-term rentals
- How to stage a vacant listing that sells

## Batch E — regional / trilingual (defensible white space)
- Interior design Yerevan (AM + RU versions of the drafted EN page)
- Online interior design for the CIS / Russian-speaking clients (RU)
- Furniture shopping in Armenia: where to actually buy the look

## Cadence & mechanics
- **2–4 posts/month** is plenty to compound; consistency beats volume.
- Refresh the "best tools" + "cost" posts **quarterly** (dates/prices) — freshness feeds ranking + AI citation.
- After publishing, submit URLs in Google Search Console; add internal links from `/ai-vision`, `/pricing`, `/services`.
- Cut a **vertical before/after image per post** for Pinterest (see setup checklist).
- Track which posts drive free-tier signups; double down on the winners.
