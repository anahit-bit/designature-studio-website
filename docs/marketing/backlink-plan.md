## 0. Read first — how this works & the one rule

**Backlinks** = other websites linking to designature.studio. Google treats each relevant, credible
link as a vote; more good votes → better rankings and more visibility to AI answer engines.

**Referral vs. SEO (the thing Anahit asked about):** a "referral" link (e.g. a partner's site linking
to us) does two jobs at once — sends *visitors* AND passes *SEO authority*. So partner/referral
relationships are a backlink source. Caveat: links between two **direct competitors** are unusual and
Google may discount pure reciprocal "link swaps," so don't over-rely on them — the biggest wins come
from **non-competing, relevant** sites (design blogs, real-estate sites, press, directories).

**THE ONE RULE: earn or request links from relevant, real sites. Never buy links, never use PBNs or
link farms, never spam comments/forums, never mass-submit to junk directories.** Google penalizes
that. Relevance and quality beat volume every time. (nofollow links still count — they drive traffic,
brand searches, and AI citations even when they don't pass full "link juice.")

## The plan — 4 tiers, in priority order

Each item is tagged **[SESSION]** (this session can do it now) or **[OWNER]** (needs Anahit's accounts,
inbox, or sign-off). The session should *prepare* everything so the owner only has to approve/submit.

### TIER 1 — Foundation links (fast, high-certainty)
Get the easy, legitimate citations every real business should have.
1. **AI-tool directories** [SESSION builds list + listing copy → OWNER submits]:
   There's An AI For That, Futurepedia, Toolify, Insidr, AIxploria, ToolPilot, AI Chief, OpenTools,
   plus **Product Hunt**, **Capterra**, **G2**, **Crunchbase**. (Competitors' listings here are young —
   we can match them fast.)
2. **Design & home directories** [SESSION list → OWNER]: Houzz professional profile, Behance/Dribbble
   portfolio, Dezeen/ArchDaily directories where free, relevant "find a designer" listings.
3. **Business & local (Armenia/Yerevan)** [OWNER, session drafts]: Google Business Profile, Bing
   Places, Apple Business Connect, Yerevan/Armenia business directories and **.am** listings, Armenian
   tech-ecosystem directories. High relevance, low competition — a real local-SEO win.
4. **Owned profile links** [OWNER]: LinkedIn company page, Pinterest, YouTube, Medium, Instagram/Facebook
   bio links — all pointing to the right pages.

### TIER 2 — Linkable assets (earn links passively)
Publish things other people *want* to link to.
5. **"State of AI Interior Design 2026" report page** [SESSION builds] — turn our 96-tool dataset into a
   public, citable page (key stats, the pricing landscape, "we analyzed 96 tools"). Data pages earn
   links and get quoted by AI. See Task 3.
6. **The GEO articles + guides** [OWNER publishes via journal session] — comparison + how-to content
   earns links over time; the "virtual staging cost" and "MLS disclosure rules" pieces are especially
   reference-able.
7. **A small free tool** [SESSION can scope/build later] — e.g. a "virtual staging cost calculator" or
   "deposit-safe rental checklist." Simple utilities attract links.

### TIER 3 — Outreach links (highest authority, needs sending)
8. **Journalist-query platforms** [SESSION drafts responses → OWNER sends]: **Connectively (ex-HARO),
   Featured, Qwoted, SourceBottle.** Anahit answers reporter queries as an interior-design/AI expert →
   earns links from real publications. Highest ROI outreach; low effort per win.
9. **Guest posts / expert contributions** [SESSION drafts pitches + outlines → OWNER sends]: design
   blogs, real-estate/agent blogs (for the staging angle), startup & **Armenian tech press** (founder
   story). One good guest post = one strong contextual link + audience.
10. **Unlinked-mention reclamation** [SESSION finds → OWNER asks]: find pages that mention "Designature"
    but don't link us, and request a link. Easiest "yes" in outreach.
11. **Competitor backlink intersection** [SESSION builds list → OWNER pitches]: find who links to
    MeltFlex / REimagine / Havenly (roundups, "best tools" lists, resource pages) and pitch them to add
    Designature. These sites already link to our category — warm targets.

### TIER 4 — Partner / referral links (relationship-based)
12. **Mutual referrals** [OWNER, with session-drafted terms]: the MeltFlex referral (we send DIY users,
    they send human-design seekers) can include a link where genuine. Also: real-estate agencies /
    developers we do staging for, furniture partners, and any affiliate relationships that offer a
    partner/"as featured in" page. Keep these genuine and relevant — not bulk swaps.

## Tracking & cadence
- Keep everything in `backlink-targets.csv`; update `Status` (to-do / submitted / live / declined).
- Realistic pace: a handful of new referring domains per month compounds fast for a young site.
- **Monthly:** submit the next batch, send 5–10 outreach emails, publish/refresh one linkable asset.
- **Report monthly:** new referring domains, estimated DR/DA trend, any press wins, and rankings for
  target keywords (see the content calendar for the keyword list).

## Success metrics
Referring domains ↑ · Domain Rating/Authority ↑ · rankings for "AI interior design / virtual staging /
interior design Yerevan" ↑ · referral traffic from links · presence in AI-answer citations.

## Guardrails (repeat)
No bought links, no PBNs, no link farms, no comment/forum spam, no junk-directory blasts. Every link
must come from a real, relevant site. Prefer 10 relevant links over 100 junk ones.
