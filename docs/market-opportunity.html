Where this business is going (and why it's fundable)

The 5-year view (to 2030): the AI-design market roughly doubles to ~$4–5B, the category consolidates (more Havenly-style roll-ups and strategic acquisitions), and the winning model is clear — commerce attach (shoppable) + B2B/real-estate distribution + a human-quality layer pure-AI tools can't match. Designature can win a global niche in hybrid + shoppable across the US and UK, on a realistic path to a few $M ARR and acquisition interest.

The 10-year view (to 2035): "design your room with AI and buy it" is mainstream, the AI slice is ~$11–15B, and generative AI is everywhere (~$1T). Value accrues to whoever owns proprietary data, distribution, or trust — not to another render tool. Exit is most likely a strategic acquisition by a retailer, building-products, or proptech player (like Yardzen→CRH), with IPO possible only at platform scale (like Manycore).

Why we're the fundable version, not the Modsy version: AI does the cost-heavy work (cheap renders), humans appear only as a premium, margin-accretive layer, and we monetize the furniture sale (affiliate) plus virtual staging — so unit economics don't depend on burning VC to pay designers. That's the exact mistake that killed the $72M cautionary tale.
