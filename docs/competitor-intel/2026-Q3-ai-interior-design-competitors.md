## 1. The one-paragraph read

The pure "upload a photo → AI restyle" capability is now a **commodity** — 15+ tools do it,
a free tier of ~3 designs is table stakes, and per-render cost has collapsed to $0.05–$0.30.
Competing on render volume is a race to the bottom. The **defensible ground is the wrapper**
around the AI: a curated *shoppable* list of real products, plus a *human* designer, at prices
the AI-only crowd can't match with service and the human-only crowd can't match on price.
That exact middle — **shoppable + human, at AI prices** — is under-occupied. Designature's
$0/$19/$49 + $99 ladder, the Style Quiz (nearly unique), and a bilingual Armenian/Russian/English
remote footprint sit in white space no single competitor currently owns.

## 2. Where the white space is (and the two real risks)

**White space Designature can own:**
1. **Shoppable + human at AI prices.** Tools that do shoppable **and** human (Spacejoy $499–$999,
   Havenly $199–$699, Decorilla $299+) are premium and human-only. Tools that do shoppable **and**
   cheap (REimagine, MeltFlex, MyRoomDesigner, HomeDesigns Furniture Finder) are AI-only with no
   human layer. Nobody bridges both cheaply.
2. **The Style Quiz as onboarding moat.** Almost every competitor jumps straight to a render.
   A quiz-led, personalized entry point is rare and doubles as lead capture.
3. **Bilingual, productized, remote — for the CIS/Armenia neighborhood.** Regional tools are either
   bare planners (Planoplan, RU) or DIY apps with Russian UI (Planner 5D); local Yerevan/Tbilisi
   studios are bespoke and offline. "Armenian studio, AR/RU/EN, AI + human, remote-worldwide" is unclaimed.

**The two real risks:**
- **"Free" has been normalized by giants.** IKEA Kreativ, Wayfair Decorify, Amazon "View in Your Room,"
  and Houzz already trained consumers to expect *upload → shoppable redesign* at $0 — but all are
  **catalog-locked** to their own SKUs. Designature's neutral, curated, multi-retailer list is the
  contrast, but the taste/curation must visibly justify a price against free.
- **The graveyard.** Modsy (2022), Decorist (2022), Laurel & Wolf (2019), Homepolish (2019) and Hutch
  (pivoted) all died on the same thing: **human-design margins / CAC burn on cheap flat-fee design.**
  The structural hedge is exactly Designature's shape — cheap AI does the concept, humans appear only
  at the $99 tier — plus capturing the furniture sale (affiliate/markup on the Shopping List), which
  every survivor (Havenly, Decorilla, Spacejoy) monetizes.

## 4. The field by segment

Full per-company detail (pricing, covers, gaps) is in the CSV/XLSX/HTML. Summary:

### Hybrid AI + Human e-design — *your model* (7 tracked; closest)
Havenly (5), Decorilla (5), Spacejoy (4), The Expert (3, **acquired by Havenly Feb 2026**),
Yardzen (2, outdoor), Michael Helwig Interiors (3, indie long-tail), Nousdecor (likely dormant).
**Takeaway:** only Havenly truly mirrors the free-AI + human + retail stack, and it's priced 4–10×
higher with no micro-tiers. This is the segment to defend hardest.

### AI redesign + shoppable — *the wedge* (6 tracked; head-to-head)
REimagine Home (5), MeltFlex AI (5), MyRoomDesigner.ai (5), Decoratly (4), Paintit.ai (4),
HomeDesigns.ai Furniture Finder (4). **Takeaway:** this is your differentiator's actual battleground.
None have a human layer — that's your separation.

### Pure AI redesign tools (15 tracked; commoditizing)
RoomGPT, Interior AI, Spacely AI, Collov, ArchitectGPT, ReRoom, mnml.ai, DreamHouse, Remodel AI,
Remodeled AI, DecorAI, GenRoom, RoomsGPT, MyArchitectAI, AI Room Planner.
**Takeaway:** render is a commodity; free ~3 designs is standard; entry $19–29, pro $49–99.
Collov literally matches your $19/$49. Don't compete here on price — compete on the wrapper.

### Mobile apps (7 tracked)
DecorMatters (4, 10M users, shoppable + social), Homestyler, Planner 5D, SofaBrain, Room Planner (Ltd),
Roomify, Home Design 3D, MagicPlan. **Takeaway:** predatory weekly-sub funnels ($4.99–$6.99/wk) and
app-store discovery; Designature is web-first with no app — a discovery/monetization gap worth weighing.

### Retailer / shop-the-look commerce (5 tracked; free but locked)
IKEA Kreativ (4), Wayfair Decorify (4), Amazon View in Your Room (2), Pinterest Shop the Look (2),
Roomvo/Leap Tools (2, flooring). **Takeaway:** free and well-funded, but single-brand. Your neutrality wins.

### Retailer free design services (7 tracked; the "why pay?" pressure)
West Elm, Room & Board, Crate & Barrel, CB2, Pottery Barn, Ethan Allen, Bassett — all free human design,
monetized via furniture. **Takeaway:** catalog-captive, no generative AI, no brand-agnostic advice.

### AI virtual staging (8 tracked; adjacent, converging)
Virtual Staging AI (Zillow), ApplyDesign, BoxBrownie, Styldod, roOomy, Stuccco, PhotoUp, AI HomeDesign.
**Takeaway:** sell to *agents*, not homeowners — low direct threat, but Styldod's own REimagineHome shows
staging players bolting on shoppability. Watch the convergence.

### Pro / design software (23 tracked; tools, not services)
Foyr Neo, Planner 5D, Coohom, Homestyler, SketchUp AI Render, Cedreo, Live Home 3D, RoomSketcher,
HomeByMe, Morpholio, Chief Architect, 2020 Design, DesignFiles, Programa, Mydoma, Fohlio, Maket.ai,
Finch 3D, TestFit, Modelo, Spoak, Archilogic, Houzz Pro. **Takeaway:** mostly "you build it yourself."
Spoak (4) is the closest *positioning* analog; HomeByMe validates the tool+human-service hybrid.

### Regional / emerging (8 tracked)
Gepetto (FR), Planoplan (RU/CIS), DeHome (HK), Livspace/HomeLane/Bonito (India, execution-heavy),
Dubai/MENA cluster, Rooms Studio & local Yerevan/Tbilisi studios. **Takeaway:** the geographic +
language + productized-remote intersection is empty — your clearest local moat.

### Horizontal generative AI (3) & Adjacent (2)
Freepik, Krea, Leonardo.ai (DIY substitutes); Interior Define, Swatchbox. Low threat, watch as substitutes.

### Defunct / precedent (5) — the warnings
Modsy, Decorist, Laurel & Wolf, Homepolish, Hutch. All died on human-design economics. Learn the lesson;
don't repeat it.

## 5. Recommendations (from this quarter's read)

1. **Message the wrapper, not the render.** Lead with "shoppable + a real designer," not "AI redesign."
   The render is table stakes; say what's different.
2. **Make the Shopping List monetizable.** Affiliate/markup on the list is how every survivor stays alive —
   and it lets the AI tiers stay cheap without bleeding.
3. **Defend against "free" with curation and neutrality.** Show the multi-retailer, best-regardless-of-store
   value that IKEA/Wayfair/Amazon structurally can't offer.
4. **Own the CIS/Armenia lane loudly.** Bilingual (AM/RU/EN), remote-worldwide, AI + human — no one else has it.
5. **Weigh a mobile presence.** The app-store funnel is where DecorMatters (10M) and the weekly-sub crowd live;
   web-only forgoes that discovery.
6. **Watch REimagine Home, MeltFlex, MyRoomDesigner most closely** — they're closing on the shoppable edge.
