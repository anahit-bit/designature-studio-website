# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-06-17
**Last updated:** 2026-06-17

This Privacy Policy explains how Anahit Ghasabyan IE ("Designature Studio", "we", "our", "us"), the operator of Designature AI Studio at www.designature.studio (the "Service"), collects, uses, and shares information about you when you use the Service.

We aim to be straightforward about what we collect and why. If you have any questions, please contact us at **hello@designature.studio**.

## 1. Who We Are

Designature Studio is operated by Anahit Ghasabyan, a sole-proprietor business (Individual Entrepreneur / IE) registered in the Republic of Armenia. Registered address: Demirchyan 31, Apt 26, Yerevan, Armenia.

For the purposes of data-protection law, we are the data controller for the personal information we collect about users of the Service. Where our Merchant of Record (currently Paddle) acts as the billing party, the MoR is the controller for billing-related data — see their privacy policy for details.

## 2. Information We Collect

### Information you give us directly

- **Account information**: your email address, display name, and profile photo (if you sign in with Google)
- **Subscription information**: which tier you subscribe to and your billing period
- **Content you upload**: images of rooms you upload for AI Vision or Room Audit, text prompts you enter, and selections you make in the Style Quiz
- **Communications**: messages you send us at hello@designature.studio or through any support form

### Information we collect automatically

- **Usage data**: which tools you use, how often, generation counts, and timestamps — used to enforce subscription quotas and improve the Service
- **Device and log data**: IP address, browser type, referring URL, and approximate location derived from IP — collected for security, abuse prevention, and basic analytics
- **Cookies and similar technologies**: session cookies for login, preference cookies for your settings, and analytics cookies (see Section 6)

### Information we receive from third parties

- **From Google** (if you sign in with Google): your verified email address, name, profile picture, and a Google account identifier — used only to create and authenticate your account
- **From Paddle** (after you subscribe): your subscription status, plan, billing period, and a Paddle customer identifier — used to grant you access at the correct tier

We do not see or store your full payment card details at any point. Paddle handles all payment information.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

- Provide, operate, and maintain the Service
- Authenticate you and keep your account secure
- Process AI generations and deliver outputs to your browser
- Enforce subscription quotas and feature gates
- Communicate with you about the Service (account notices, security alerts, transactional emails)
- Respond to support requests
- Improve the Service (analytics, debugging, identifying issues)
- Detect, prevent, and address fraud, abuse, or violations of our Terms
- Comply with legal obligations

We do **not** sell your personal information.

## 4. Third-Party Processors

To run the Service we rely on a small set of trusted providers. Each receives only the data needed to do its job:

- **Google (OAuth)** — verifies your Google identity when you sign in
- **Google Gemini API** — receives the image or text prompt you submit, returns the AI-generated output
- **Cloudinary** — stores and delivers images, including AI-generated outputs and uploaded photos
- **Paddle.com Market Limited** — processes payments, manages subscriptions, handles invoices and tax compliance (Merchant of Record)
- **Resend** — sends transactional emails (account confirmations, payment receipts, support replies)
- **Google Analytics 4** — provides anonymous, aggregated usage analytics
- **Railway** — hosts the application servers
- **Sanity** — hosts the public portfolio content (does not see subscriber data)
- **Serper / web-search providers** — when you use Shopping List, your search query is sent to a search API to fetch retailer results

Each provider is bound by its own privacy and security commitments. We share only what is necessary.

## 5. AI Generation and Your Uploads

When you submit a prompt or image to one of our AI tools, the content is transmitted to the underlying AI provider (currently Google's Gemini API) for processing. AI providers may temporarily retain inputs and outputs to monitor for abuse or improve their service, in accordance with their own policies.

We do not use your uploads to train any AI model. We retain your generated outputs in your account so you can revisit them; you can delete them from your account at any time.

## 6. Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies for:

- **Authentication** — to keep you signed in
- **Preferences** — to remember settings such as the active billing interval on the pricing page
- **Analytics** — Google Analytics 4 measures aggregate usage to help us improve the Service

Most browsers let you control cookies through their settings. Blocking essential cookies will prevent you from staying signed in.

We do not run third-party advertising trackers on the Service.

## 7. Data Retention

We retain your account information for as long as your account is active. After you delete your account, we delete your personal data within 30 days, except where we are required to keep certain records for tax, legal, or fraud-prevention purposes (in which case we keep only the minimum required and isolate it from active systems).

Billing records held by Paddle are retained according to Paddle's policies, typically for the period required by tax law.

Server logs (IP, timestamps, error traces) are retained for up to 90 days for security and debugging.

## 8. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

- **Access** — request a copy of the data we hold about you
- **Correction** — ask us to fix inaccurate or incomplete data
- **Deletion** — ask us to delete your data (subject to legal retention requirements)
- **Portability** — ask us to provide your data in a machine-readable format
- **Objection / restriction** — ask us to stop or restrict certain processing
- **Withdraw consent** — where we process data based on your consent, you may withdraw it at any time

To exercise any of these rights, email us at **hello@designature.studio**. We respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you may also lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority.

## 9. International Data Transfers

Our service providers operate in multiple countries, including the United States and the European Union. When your data is transferred outside of Armenia, we rely on the safeguards offered by those providers, such as standard contractual clauses or equivalent mechanisms.

## 10. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including HTTPS for all traffic, encrypted credential storage, restricted server access, and regular updates of our dependencies. No system is perfectly secure; if we ever become aware of a security incident affecting your data, we will notify you promptly.

## 11. Children

The Service is not intended for users under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

## 12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify active users by email or in-app notice. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page indicates the most recent revision.
