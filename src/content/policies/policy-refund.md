The sections below apply to **credits** — both one-time credit packs and the monthly credit subscription.

## 1. One-Time Credit Packs

**Yes — you can request a refund on a one-time credit pack** (Starter, Project, or Project Plus). A pack is a single payment that adds credits to your account immediately, and purchased credits never expire.

**Your refund right:**

- **Within 14 calendar days of purchase, for any reason.** If you have not spent the credits, we refund the pack in full and remove the credits from your account.
- **If you have already spent some of the credits**, we refund the value of the **unused** credits — pro-rated to what you paid — within that same 14-day window.
- **Outside the 14-day window**, refunds are no longer automatic, but you can still ask. We consider genuine cases at our discretion (see Section 3).
- **If a charge succeeds but the credits are not delivered** to your account because of a technical fault, we always make it right — we either deliver the credits or refund you in full, your choice, with no time limit.

**How to request a pack refund:**

1. Email **hello@designature.studio** from the email address on your account, with the subject line **"Credit refund"**.
2. Tell us which purchase — the date and amount, or the receipt we emailed you.
3. We reply within **3 business days**, confirm the amount, and process it.
4. The refund is returned to your original card through Ameriabank, and any refunded credits are removed from your balance (see Section 4 for timing).

## 2. Monthly Subscription

The monthly subscription is $49 USD per month for 1,000 credits, and renews automatically until you cancel.

**First-time 14-day window.** If you subscribe for the **first time**, you may request a full refund within **14 calendar days** of your initial payment, for any reason. This matches the consumer-cancellation right granted by EU and UK consumer-protection law for digital services; we extend it to all subscribers worldwide.

**Cancelling.** You can cancel at any time from your account or by emailing us. After cancellation:

- Your subscription stays active until the end of the current paid month, and you keep your remaining monthly credits until then.
- At the end of that period, the subscription stops, no further charge is made, and your account reverts to Free. Any unused monthly credits from the subscription expire at that point.
- **Credits you bought in one-time packs are not affected** and remain in your account.
- We do not provide partial refunds for the unused portion of a current paid month, except where required by law or under Sections 1, 2, or 3.

**Renewals.** An automatic renewal is a continuation of your subscription, not a new first-time purchase, so the 14-day window in this Section does not reset on renewal. To avoid an unwanted renewal, cancel **before** your renewal date. If a renewal charges because you forgot to cancel and you contact us within **7 calendar days** of the renewal, we will normally refund it as a courtesy, provided you have not substantially used that month's credits.

## 3. Discretionary Refunds

Outside the windows above, refunds are not automatic, but we may grant them at our discretion in cases such as:

- Accidental duplicate charges
- A charge that occurs after a cancellation request was submitted but before it took effect
- A material defect in the Service that we cannot reasonably fix within a reasonable time

To request a discretionary refund, email **hello@designature.studio** describing the situation. We will respond within 7 business days.

## 4. How Refunds Are Issued

Approved refunds are returned to the original card used for the purchase, through Ameriabank. Processing usually takes 5–10 business days to appear on your statement, depending on your card issuer. Where a refund reverses credits already added to your account, those credits are removed as part of the refund.

## 5. Exclusions

We will not refund where:

- The account has been used in violation of our Terms of Service
- The charge is being disputed through your card issuer (a chargeback) rather than with us first — please contact us first; chargebacks bypass this Policy and may result in account closure
- The request is made outside the windows above and Section 3 does not apply
