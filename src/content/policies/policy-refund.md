# Refund Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-06-17
**Last updated:** 2026-06-17

This Refund Policy applies to subscriptions to Designature AI Studio at www.designature.studio, operated by Anahit Ghasabyan IE ("Designature Studio", "we", "us"). It forms part of our [Terms of Service](https://www.designature.studio/terms).

All paid subscriptions are processed by our Merchant of Record, currently **Paddle.com Market Limited ("Paddle")**. Refunds are issued by the MoR on our behalf.

## Paid Consultation ($99)

The 45-minute paid design consultation is a one-time service, processed securely by our payment provider to the card you used.

- Reschedule or cancel free up to 24 hours before your booked start time for a full refund.
- Cancellations within 24 hours of the start time, or no-shows, are non-refundable.
- If we cancel or cannot hold the call, you receive a full refund or a free reschedule — your choice.
- If you book a design project with us within 30 days of your consultation, the $99 is credited toward that project.

To request a consultation refund, email **hello@designature.studio** from the email address you booked with, subject line "Consultation refund".

The sections below apply to AI Studio subscriptions.

## 1. 14-Day Refund Window for New Subscribers

If you subscribe to a paid plan for the **first time**, you may request a full refund within **14 calendar days** of your initial payment, for any reason.

This window matches the consumer-cancellation right granted by EU and UK consumer-protection law for digital services. We extend it to all subscribers worldwide regardless of where you live.

To request a refund, email **hello@designature.studio** from the email address on your account with the subject line "Refund request" within the 14-day window. We will confirm and instruct Paddle to issue the refund.

## 2. After the 14-Day Window

After the 14-day window for first-time subscribers, refunds are no longer automatic. We may still grant refunds at our discretion in cases such as:

- A material defect in the Service that we cannot reasonably fix within a reasonable time
- Accidental duplicate charges
- Charges that occur after a cancellation request was submitted but before it took effect

To request a discretionary refund, email **hello@designature.studio** describing the situation. We will respond within 7 business days.

## 3. Renewals

When your subscription renews automatically at the end of a billing period, the renewal is treated as a continuation of your subscription, not a new first-time purchase. The 14-day refund window in Section 1 does not reset on renewal.

You can avoid an unwanted renewal by cancelling **before** the renewal date through Paddle's customer portal, or by emailing us at hello@designature.studio. Once cancelled, your access continues until the end of the current paid period.

If a renewal charge occurs because you forgot to cancel and you contact us within 7 calendar days of the renewal, we will normally refund the renewal as a courtesy, provided you have not made substantial use of the Service since the renewal.

## 4. Cancellation Without Refund

You can cancel your subscription at any time through Paddle's customer portal or by emailing us at hello@designature.studio. After cancellation:

- Your access continues until the end of the current billing period you have already paid for
- At the end of that period, your account reverts to the Free tier
- We do not provide partial refunds for the unused portion of a current billing period, except where required by law or covered by Section 1 or 2 above

## 5. Annual Plans

Annual plans receive the same 14-day first-time refund window as monthly plans. Beyond 14 days, annual plans are non-refundable for the remainder of the year except under Section 2.

You can downgrade or cancel an annual plan at any time through Paddle's customer portal or by emailing us at hello@designature.studio; the change takes effect at the end of the current annual period.

## 6. Upgrades and Downgrades

You can upgrade from Design to Studio at any time — typically by clicking Subscribe on the Studio tier from the pricing page, or through Paddle's customer portal. When the upgrade happens mid-period, Paddle prorates the difference and charges only the remainder. Upgrades are not refundable separately; you can request a refund of the entire upgraded subscription within 14 days of the upgrade only if it is your first-time purchase of the higher tier.

You can downgrade from Studio to Design at any time through Paddle's customer portal or by emailing us at hello@designature.studio. Downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing period. We do not refund the difference for the remainder of the current period when you downgrade.

## 7. How Refunds Are Issued

Refunds are processed by Paddle to the original payment method used for the subscription. Processing usually takes 5–10 business days to appear in your account, depending on your card issuer or payment method.

## 8. Exclusions

We will not refund subscriptions where:

- The account has been used in violation of our Terms of Service
- The account is being chargebacked through the card issuer rather than through us (please contact us first; chargebacks bypass this Policy and may result in account closure)
- The request is made outside the windows above and Section 2 does not apply
